# The Voice — Bloodlines style bible

Every word the player reads is a page of Patient 0413's file, written as it
happens. The narrator writes to stay real: putting a thing on paper is how
they keep being the one who puts things on paper. The institution writes
back — it softens, corrects, redacts. The page is contested ground. That
contest IS the aesthetic; every device below is one side of it winning a
word.

## The narrator

- **Counts.** Steps, doors, breaths, the notes of a lullaby. Numbers are
  the last neutral things in the building.
- **Reads everything.** Labels, stitching, laundry stamps, brass plates,
  the spines of files. Reading is how they check the world is still
  printed.
- **Reports the body, never the feeling.** Not "I am afraid" — "My hands
  have decided something I have not."
- **Has a past** that surfaces only in similes, at most once or twice per
  wing, never explained: *like a child at a funeral*; *the way my mother
  stood when the phone rang.*
- **Maintains identity by repetition.** "I am the one who came in."
  Variants allowed. The phrase is a handhold, and the player should feel
  it slipping when it changes.

## The place

One hospital, every decade at once — the deeper the wing, the older the
decade. Draw from this fabric instead of inventing generic detail:

- green paint to shoulder height; plaster the color of old teeth above it
- terrazzo floors with a path worn pale down the middle
- wired glass; in the deep wings, windows painted to look like windows
- fluorescent hum; one tube always failing somewhere out of sight
- radiators that knock like something asking; trolley wheels; doors with
  rubber seals that close soft as jars
- carbolic soap; boiled greens from a kitchen no one has found; dust
  burning on the radiators
- the desk; the east mirror; the laundry stamp of Ward II; forms with the
  boxes already checked
- clocks that disagree

## Rhythm

- **Vary the gait.** A long sentence that keeps adjusting itself, then a
  short one that refuses to. A page of nothing but five-word declaratives
  is a monotone — that is the failure mode this document exists to kill.
- The narrator goes **long when avoiding, short when caught.**
- Patients speak **without quotation marks**, and their speech is allowed
  to infect the narration that follows it.
- **One image per beat.** Two images is a tie; cut the weaker one.

## Bans

- *something / somehow* as a mystery-noun — permitted only as an audible
  act of refusal ("!!Something in me does.!!"), and rarely
- *seems, appears, I notice, I realize, I feel*
- weather of the soul: "the air grows heavy", "tension fills the room"
- explained emotion; therapy vocabulary; modern slang
- three fragments in a row, more than once per scene
- author-voice leaks ("the player notes…")

## The marks

**`~~strike~~`** — the document correcting itself. Three legal uses:
1. the institution softening the true word: *~~beat his daughters~~
   enforced expectations physically*
2. the narrator flinching from the true word and writing the bearable
   one: *~~My name.~~ The card I will not be needing.*
3. a tense correction where time itself is wrong: *~~I will not~~ I have
   not turned left. Yet.*
The struck text must DIFFER from what follows — the player reads both,
and the gap between them is the content. A strike that repeats itself
verbatim is a stutter, not a correction. (Budget: at most one deliberate
verbatim repeat per patient, at the single most charged moment, where
writing it, striking it, and writing it again IS the point.)

**`[[N]]`** — a redaction bar N characters wide (N is a WIDTH, not text —
`[[1973]]` renders a 1,973-char bar). Someone else got to the page first.
The narrator never redacts themselves. Use on names, dates, the contents
of containers. The bar should sit where the worst word goes; imagination
fills it in worse than any word would.

**`**gold**`** — the wrong word glowing. Institutional euphemism at its
most polished: **rested**, **volunteers**, **preserved**, **expected**.
Small words only, inside body text, never a whole sentence. The player
should notice it and wonder who chose it.

**`!!blood!!`** — the page bleeding through. Direct threat, standing
orders, the narrator's voice cracking, the moment the clinical tone
breaks. If it appears on a routine line it stops meaning anything.

## Tests for a line

1. **Could it appear in any horror game?** Then it isn't finished.
   Sharpen until it could only appear in this one.
2. **Can you draw it?** If not, it is obtuse, not cryptic. Cryptic means
   precise about a wrong thing; obtuse means vague about nothing.
3. **Does the strangeness have manners?** The horror here is polite,
   procedural, patient. It fills in forms. It waits to be reached for.
4. **Read it after a flat line.** If the gait doesn't change, recut it.

## Calibration — before / after

> **Before:** The station is lit from below. A nurse I have not seen
> before is at the desk.
>
> **After:** The nurses' station is lit from underneath, the way you
> light a face to make it a mask. A nurse I have not met is on duty.
> There has always been a nurse I have not met.

> **Before:** I take it. It is warm. The room is steadier for a moment.
>
> **After:** I eat it standing up, like a child at a funeral. It is warm
> all the way down. ~~I am steadier.~~ The room is steadier. One of us
> is.

> **Before:** I keep walking. The rattling goes on a long time.
>
> **After:** I keep walking. The rattle keeps time with my steps for
> longer than the box stays in sight. ~~Behind me.~~ In my chest. I pay
> it no attention, which is a way of paying.
